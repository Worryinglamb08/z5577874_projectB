# Out-of-sample minimum-CVaR prototype comparison

## Note
Minimum CVaR minimises the average loss beyond the trailing 95% loss threshold. It is compared with Minimum Variance and Hierarchical Risk Parity using identical monthly dates, constraints and trading costs. Daily CVaR and drawdown are shown as positive loss magnitudes.

## Sample
2021-01-01 to 2023-12-31 (family-specific observed calendars)

## Units
Sharpe ratio and percentage loss, drawdown, and turnover
