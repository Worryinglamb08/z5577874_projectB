# Out-of-sample net Sharpe ratio by fund family and method

## Note
Bars compare the fifteen primary monthly funds after the 10 basis-point turnover-cost assumption. Sharpe uses a 0% annual risk-free rate. Equity and Combined use 252-day annualisation; Crypto uses 365-day annualisation. Higher historical Sharpe is not a forecast of future performance.
