# Coverage confidence across equity-sector sentiment indices

## Note
Confidence equals covered-company breadth multiplied by one minus headline-share HHI, scaled relative to an equal five-company sector and clipped to [0, 1]. Zero means no usable news evidence; values near one indicate broad, comparatively even coverage. Confidence measures evidence support, not whether the sentiment classification is correct.

## Sample
2020-01-02 to 2023-12-29

## Units
Confidence score on [0, 1]
