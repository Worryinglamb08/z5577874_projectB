# Ideas If Time Permits

## Rebrand Stockist Funds as Stoicist Funds

### Status

Deferred idea. Do not change the current Stockist Funds implementation unless
the rebrand is explicitly approved later.

### Concept

Rename **Stockist Funds** to **Stoicist Funds**, inspired by the Stoic principle
that external events cannot be controlled, but our response to them can be.
This aligns with the product's systematic approach: markets cannot be controlled
or predicted reliably, but portfolio rules, diversification, constraints,
trading costs and monthly rebalancing can be managed deliberately.

Possible brand line:

> **Stoicist Funds — Control the process, not the market.**

Supporting value proposition:

> Systematic funds designed around disciplined responses to uncertain markets,
> with historical evidence, risks and implementation assumptions kept visible.

### Brief compatibility

The project brief permits the student to choose the app's name. It states that
the student will give the app a name and only provides `UNSWTrader` as an
example. Station 1 also requires the app to be named and its value proposition
explained. It does not require one prescribed brand or prohibit refinement of
the name between Parts A and B.

If adopted, the Part B report should explain the development clearly:

> The Project A concept, Stockist Funds, was rebranded as Stoicist Funds in
> Part B to better express its disciplined, rules-based response to
> uncontrollable market outcomes.
