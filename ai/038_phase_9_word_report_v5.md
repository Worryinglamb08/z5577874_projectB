# Phase 9 — Word report Version 5

## Goal

Reframe the report's recommendations as evidence-conditioned directions for
further investigation and add a carefully governed regime-aware research path.

## Student prompts

> I think the overall tone should shift from: “This is what the product should
> do.” to: “Given these results, this would be a reasonable direction to
> investigate next.”

> I'd like to recommend regime specific model as well, a lot of geopolitics
> plays into market pricing

> Create a V5 with these recommendations

## Changes made

- Created `report/report_v5.docx`; Versions 2, 3 and 4 were not overwritten.
- Renamed Section 6.3 to **Three evidence-conditioned recommendations** and
  added an explicit statement that the recommendations are research directions,
  not product prescriptions from a three-year historical simulation.
- Replaced the combined-fund product directive with a frozen forward-test
  recommendation covering all 15 funds, the approved lexicon, costs and signal
  rules. It uses one predeclared primary measure with drawdown and turnover
  guardrails.
- Updated the sentiment recommendation to acknowledge the completed human
  review. It now proposes company, sector and market relevance classification,
  cross-ticker repetition checks and a frozen holdout test.
- Added a regime-aware recommendation that combines estimation stability with
  two or three interpretable regimes based on lagged trend, volatility,
  drawdown, cross-asset correlation and geopolitical-news intensity. It rejects
  retrospective regime labels and treats geopolitical news as risk intensity,
  not a directional forecast.
- Updated the conclusion to point to frozen evidence, headline relevance,
  regime sensitivity and user-comprehension testing rather than an unfinished
  text audit.
- Updated the cover, metadata and AI-use disclosure to Version 5.
- No analytical artifacts or figures changed, and no PDF was created.

## Reasoning

The brief asks for exactly three concrete recommendations. Forward validation,
headline relevance, and estimation/regime stability were retained as the three
recommendations because they follow directly from the short sample, the manual
headline overrides, and the unstable allocation evidence. User-comprehension
testing remains in the limitations and conclusion rather than becoming a fourth
recommendation.
